Crime Impact

Image-based sexual abuse is not only a personal violation. It is also a cybercrime issue because digital tools can increase the speed, scale, and permanence of the abuse. Online platforms, messaging apps, cloud storage, and artificial intelligence can all affect how the crime occurs and spreads.
