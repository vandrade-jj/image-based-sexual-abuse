This project examines the sharing, posting, or threatening to share private sexual images without consent. The crime is not about a victim's choice to trust someone; it is about an offender using digital access, power, and exposure to harm another person.

Crime Definition

Nonconsensual distribution of intimate images occurs when private nude, sexual, or intimate images are shared or threatened to be shared without the depicted person's permission. It is one specific form of image-based sexual abuse (Shapiro, 2023).

Why It Happens Online

Phones, cloud storage, social media, messaging apps, and private online groups make images easier to copy, circulate, and preserve. Once an image spreads, the victim-survivor may lose control over where it appears or who sees it.

Prevalence

Research summarized by Shapiro (2023) reports wide prevalence ranges, including 1%–30% for nonconsensual distribution and 1%–15% for threats to distribute. Underreporting is likely because of stigma, self-blame, fear, and delayed discovery.
