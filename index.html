A specific form of image-based sexual abuse

Nonconsensual Distribution of Intimate Images.

A private image can become a tool of exposure, humiliation, coercion, and control when someone shares or threatens to share it without consent.

§ 01

What is the crime?

  • Definition: Nonconsensual distribution of intimate images occurs when nude, sexual, or intimate images are shared, posted, traded, or threatened to be shared without the depicted person's permission.
  • Consent matters: Consent to create or privately send an image is not the same as consent to distribute it to others.
  • Digital harm: Phones, cloud storage, private messages, group chats, and social media allow images to be copied and circulated quickly.

§ 02

General information about the crime and criminal

  • Offenders may be current partners, ex-partners, acquaintances, online contacts, or anonymous users in private digital spaces.
  • Common motives include revenge, humiliation, coercion, control, sexual gratification, entertainment, or gaining status in an online group.
  • Victims may be blamed or shamed even though the wrongdoing is the offender’s decision to distribute or threaten the image without consent.
